FAQ Before we begin

A few things
you might wonder.

Not sure where to start? A short message is enough to get the conversation going.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

A brief description of what you need, what you want it to achieve, and any timing or budget considerations is a useful start. If you have a current website or examples you like, include those too.

Can you work on an existing website?

Yes. Website maintenance and backend development are available. Share your website address and what you would like to improve so we can see whether it is a good fit.

How are project timelines and costs worked out?

They depend on the scope and requirements. After we discuss the project, I can suggest a suitable approach before any work begins.

How does the enquiry form work?

It opens Gmail in your browser with your enquiry filled in. Sign in if needed and send the email yourself. This website does not send emails or store form submissions.
